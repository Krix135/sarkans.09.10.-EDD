⚠️ This is satire. Nestlé never actually said the lines in the speech bubbles; they're jokes. The gallon counter shows how much water a well pumping at Nestlé's 2018 Michigan permit rate (400 gallons a minute) would pump during the video. The facts in the video are real and were widely reported:
• 2018: Michigan approved Nestlé pumping up to 400 gallons of groundwater a minute in Osceola County; the state's yearly fee was about $200 (reported by the Detroit Free Press, The Guardian and others).
• 2005: then-CEO Peter Brabeck called the idea of water as a human right "extreme" in the documentary "We Feed the World". He later said his words were taken out of context.
• 2024: Nestlé admitted using filtration treatments that are banned for "natural mineral water" in France, and agreed to pay a €2 million fine to avoid a trial (reported by Reuters, Le Monde and others).
• Since 1977: the Nestlé boycott over how it marketed baby formula in poorer countries.
